J.A.R.V.I.S. Desktop - movie-style HUD assistant (Windows 10/11)

FIRST TIME
1) Install Python 3.12 (NOT 3.14):  open cmd and run  py install 3.12
2) Double-click setup.bat (once, needs internet)
3) Double-click run.bat   (run-debug.bat shows errors)

MAKE IT A REAL APP (.exe)
4) Double-click build.bat (3-6 minutes) -> dist\JARVIS\JARVIS.exe
   IMPORTANT: run JARVIS.exe from the dist folder. The build folder is temporary and its exe will not start.
5) Double-click shortcuts.bat -> Desktop icon + optional auto-start with Windows
   Share the whole dist\JARVIS folder (zip it) to give the app to someone else.

UPDATES (v3.2+)
  JARVIS checks GitHub Releases 25 s after start and then every 6 hours. When a newer version exists you see a glowing "UPDATE x.y" button next to
  the tabs, an orange dot on SETTINGS, and JARVIS tells you. Click it (or say "update now", then "yes"): the new version is downloaded, checked against its
  SHA-256 file (if the release has one), and installed; JARVIS restarts by itself and keeps your jarvis_config.json and your memory.
  Also: "check for updates", "what is new", "what version are you", Settings > CHECK FOR UPDATES, and a switch for the automatic check.
  The copy that runs from source (python jarvis.py) only opens the download page.
  FOR THE MAINTAINER: set UPDATE_REPO at the top of jarvis.py (or "update": {"repo": "owner/repository"} in jarvis_config.json) to your GitHub repository,
  run build.bat, then package-release.bat, and attach the two files it makes in the "release" folder to a new GitHub Release tagged v<version>.

GOOGLE FIRST
  Searches ask Google first (together with DuckDuckGo, Bing and Wikipedia). Safe and reliable: a free Google Programmable Search key
  ("google": {"api_key", "cx"} in jarvis_config.json, 100 free searches a day). Without it JARVIS tries to read Google's result page, which Google
  may block (JARVIS then pauses Google for 30 minutes and uses the other sources). Settings > ABOUT shows what Google is doing right now.

RESEARCH IN JARVIS'S OWN WORDS
  Ask a fact question, or say: "research electric cars", "summarize the history of bangladesh", "explain black holes in simple words".
  JARVIS asks several sources at once (Wikipedia, DuckDuckGo, Bing and more), then:
   - AI mode (free key in jarvis_config.json, see below): the model reads all the gathered notes and writes the answer entirely in its own words,
     mentions the sources by name, and says so if they disagree or do not contain the answer. "In simple words" gives a beginner explanation.
   - Basic mode (no key): merges the sentences that several sources agree on and says where they came from. This is a compressed summary,
     NOT a true rewrite; real rewording needs the AI mode. Say "tell me more" for the next part. Settings > ANSWER MODE shows which one is active.

SMART ANSWERS (no browser needed)
  Questions like "what is bangladesh", "tell me about Tesla", "weather in Dhaka" are answered by voice.
  Default: it searches the web by itself (DuckDuckGo, Bing, Wikipedia), reads the top result and answers,
  naming the source ("According to ..."). Say "tell me more" for the next part. Weather from wttr.in. Free, internet needed.
  For real AI answers to ANYTHING (free): get a free API key from Groq (console.groq.com) or Google AI Studio,
  copy jarvis_config.example.json to jarvis_config.json (same folder as jarvis.py / JARVIS.exe),
  paste the key. Your name and remembered facts are sent to that provider to personalise replies.
  "search <q>" answers inside JARVIS; say "search <q> in browser" to open Chrome instead.
  "open instagram / spotify / any-site" works; unknown names try name.com.

PHONE CALLS (Phone Link or ADB)
  Contacts: "save Rahim's number as 01712345678" (digits can be spoken: "zero one seven..."), "list my contacts", "delete contact Rahim",
  or the Memory tab > CONTACTS. Calling: "call Rahim" or "call 01712345678". JARVIS ALWAYS repeats the number and waits for "yes".
  "hang up", "answer the call". During a call he only reacts when you start with "Jarvis" (so he does not answer the person you talk to).
  Two ways, chosen in Settings > Calling method (Auto picks the best one):
   1) Phone Link (Bluetooth): JARVIS opens a tel: link, Phone Link receives the number. Needs Phone Link installed, the phone paired with
      Calls enabled, and Phone Link as the default app for "tel" links (Windows Settings > Apps > Default apps > tel). Depending on your
      Phone Link version you may have to press the green call button yourself. Hang up and answer must be done in Phone Link.
   2) ADB (phone dials by itself): install Android platform-tools, put the platform-tools folder next to JARVIS, turn on Developer options >
      USB debugging on the phone, connect by USB (or Wireless debugging) and allow this computer. Then "call", "hang up" and "answer" work from voice.
  Settings > CHECK CALLING SETUP shows what is ready. JARVIS cannot make a Bluetooth call by itself: Windows gives apps no way to do that.

FRIENDLY TALK
  "welcome to me" / "welcome me" / "greet me" greet YOU (by name if JARVIS knows it), "welcome rahim" greets a guest. Greetings use your name, the day and the time.
  Everyday sentences get a warm reply: "I am going to sleep", "I am eating", "I am studying", "I have to go", "I like you", "are you happy".
  If JARVIS does not understand he says so kindly, offers "Did you mean ...?", or offers to research it ("Say yes").

FEELINGS, WISHES AND MESSAGES
  JARVIS notices how you feel and answers like a caring friend (and the voice itself turns warmer or more cheerful):
    happy / achievements: "I won", "I passed my exam", "I got the job", "I am so happy", "my exam is tomorrow", "my friend passed"
    hard times: "I am sad", "I feel lonely", "I am stressed", "I am angry", "I can not sleep", "I failed", "my grandfather passed away", "I have a headache"
    He also reacts when you are unhappy with him ("you are useless") and responds with care if you ever say you want to hurt yourself.
  He speaks your wishes and messages to other people:
    "today is Nila's birthday", "wish Rahim happy birthday", "happy birthday to Nila", "say good luck to Sakib", "eid mubarak to everyone",
    "congratulate Rahim", "get well soon to Karim", "tell Rahim that dinner is ready", "tell Sakib he is awesome"
  AI websites: "open chatgpt" (also "chat gpt", "chatgbt"), gemini, claude, copilot, perplexity, deepseek, grok, meta ai, mistral, poe, notebooklm ... or "open an ai site".
  Network alerts: "turn on/off network alerts" in any word order.

MICROPHONE (hearing you clearly, also from far)
  The voice input was rebuilt: JARVIS now measures the room noise all the time and keeps the detection level just above it, so quiet and
  distant speech is caught and your sentence is no longer cut at the start. Quiet speech is also amplified digitally before recognition.
  Settings > MICROPHONE: input device (pick your real microphone if you have several), sensitivity (Normal / High / Far-field),
  accent (try "English (India)" for a South Asian accent), and a LIVE LEVEL BAR so you can see whether your voice reaches the mic.
  Say "I am far away" (far-field mode), "more sensitive", "less sensitive", "normal sensitivity", "set accent to Indian", "can you hear me" (mic test).
  Also raise the microphone volume in Windows sound input settings (and tick Microphone boost if offered). It helps more than anything else.
  Old versions could keep a saved Bangla setting that ruined English recognition. That is now reset to English automatically.

SPEED AND LONG IDLE (v3.1)
  Speech recognition now talks to Google over one persistent connection (no helper program to start, no new handshake per phrase).
  If it ever fails it switches back to the standard method by itself. Default pause after you stop talking: 0.65 s (Settings slider).
  The microphone is watched with a heartbeat: no audio for 8 s, or 15 s of pure silence, or a PC sleep/resume restarts it and the voice.
  Voice playback can no longer hang: it has timeouts and resets itself. Settings > "Show timing details" prints, after each voice command,
  how long speech-to-text, thinking and voice start took, so we can see exactly what is slow. Send me a screenshot of that line.
  Speed test: 4 parallel downloads for 6 s, tries Cloudflare, Hetzner, OVH, Tele2 and two CDNs, then measures upload. If every server is blocked
  by your network it tells you the exact reasons.

IF JARVIS HEARS YOU BUT DOES NOT ANSWER
  1) Say "run a self test" (or Settings > RUN SELF-TEST). He checks the microphone, wake-word mode, internet, Google speech service,
     both voices and the command engine, and shows a ticked report. Send me a screenshot of it.
  2) Wake word mode: if it is ON he only reacts when your sentence starts with "Jarvis" (he now tells you so on screen). Turn it off in Settings.
  3) If one command ever crashes, he now says so, shows the error in the Comm Log and saves details to jarvis_errors.log in your user folder
     (C:\Users\<you>\jarvis_errors.log). A command that hangs is cancelled after 45 seconds. The screen can no longer stay on PROCESSING.

RELIABILITY AND SPEED
  The microphone is watched all the time: if it stops (PC sleep, device change, stalled stream) JARVIS restarts it by himself.
  Say "restart the microphone" if it ever misbehaves. Noise level is re-calibrated after long idle.
  Warm-up at start (DNS, app list, voice) so the first command is fast. Repeated replies and questions are cached.
  The "tung" beep after your voice is OFF by default (say "beep on" if you want it back).
  Response speed: Settings page slider, or "pause" in jarvis_config.json (0.8 default; lower = faster, higher = more patient).
  Voice style (Settings): Auto / Natural / Fast. Fast uses the instant Windows voice. Auto switches to it by itself when your connection is slow.
  Windows 11 "efficiency mode" is switched off for JARVIS so he does not slow down after long idle.
  Settings > "Keep this PC awake" stops Windows from sleeping while JARVIS runs.

  "latency is unusually high". Turn on/off: "stop network alerts" / "monitor my network" or Settings.
  Ask: network status | speed test | ping google | who is connected to my wifi (scans YOUR OWN local network only) |
  what apps are using the internet | open ports | data usage | what is my ip address

APPS AND BROWSER

PRANK
  "hack the <name>" -> 38-second fake hacking screen (globe, network, password crack...) that ends with JUST A PRANK.
  ESC or click or "stop" aborts. It is only an animation; nothing real is touched.
